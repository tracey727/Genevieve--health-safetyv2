# Safety and Governance Scope

GENEVIEVE LISTENS™ V1 is a fictional-data workflow prototype. It is not a medical device, crisis service, clinical record, emergency dispatch system or autonomous suicide-prevention system.

A production version would require, at minimum:

- named clinical governance and escalation ownership;
- evidence-based content review and version control;
- structured self-harm and suicide safety evaluations;
- protections against hallucination, coercion, dependency and deceptive anthropomorphism;
- transparent model identity and limitations;
- consent, access control, encryption, retention and deletion controls;
- real service integrations that verify a named person accepted the referral;
- outage, missed-alert, adverse-event and near-miss processes;
- independent legal, privacy, accessibility and security review;
- staged testing with stop criteria and human supervision.
