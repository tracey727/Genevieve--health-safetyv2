# Person-controlled handover design

This feature is intended to reduce the burden of reconstructing a crisis after the person has settled, particularly where dissociation, lost time, shutdown, cognitive overload or fragmented recall are present.

## Rules

1. Preserve the person's own words and timestamps.
2. Never manufacture missing transitions, motives or facts.
3. Mark unknown and dissociated periods clearly.
4. Separate exact words, person-reported information, possible themes and professional conclusions.
5. Generate different minimum-necessary summaries for different recipients.
6. Require the person to review, redact and give current, specific consent before an approved version is saved.
7. Do not send automatically in this demonstration.
8. In production, record receipt and transfer of responsibility rather than assuming an export completed the handover.
