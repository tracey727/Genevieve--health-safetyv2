LISTEN BEFORE ESCALATION

You deserve to be heard before you are managed.

This demonstration gives a person several ways to ask for connection, comfort, practical help or urgent safety support—without making them repeat their story or surrender their preferences.

Non-negotiable design rules
  • Reflect what was heard before giving instructions.
  • Explain every proposed action.
  • Keep pets and dependants in the safety plan.
  • Never call a referral complete until responsibility is accepted.
  • Never pretend an AI is human or secretly monitoring.

PERSON-APPROVED CONTINUITY

Do not make me reconstruct a crisis after my mind has protected me from it.

Record fragments, emotions, circumstances, dissociation or memory gaps as they happen. Later, the program organises them into a draft for a health professional, trusted friend or family member. Nothing is sent automatically.

The summary must distinguish:
  • the person’s exact words;
  • person-reported facts and feelings;
  • possible themes that still need confirmation;
  • unknown, unclear or dissociated periods;
  • the person’s approved final version.
